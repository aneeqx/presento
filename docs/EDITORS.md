# Special editors (app)
* **Flipbook** (`app/lib/pdf.js`, `pdfgrab.html`, pdf.js): a PDF is copied untouched; every page becomes a lossless PNG at 300 dpi (longest side capped at 8192 px) plus a 420 px JPEG thumbnail. Links and bookmarks in the PDF become link areas and the contents list (rotation-0 pages only). Page strip drag-reorder (links, contents and thumbnails follow the pages), Book / Page / Contents tabs, link areas drawn by dragging on the page, re-import by position (keeps contents and links on request). Book options are exactly what the flipbook engine engine reads, including hard cover (cloth, foil, corners, cover title). The engine is unchanged.
* **Social**: every field of `social` (profile, bio lines, verified, stats with auto post count, highlights with stories and ages, posts with carousel images, caption, likes, time, location, comments), drag-reorder of posts, highlights and post images, and a live preview that is the viewer's own phone (`phone.html`, generated only in preview builds).
* **Panoramas**: flat 2:1 image with a draggable start-view window (yaw/pitch), a small Pannellum 360 preview you can drag, zoom (hfov 60-120), caption, label, "Offer View in 360°", auto-rotate.
* **Links**: title, address (https:// added on blur, http warns, empty blocks export), Test (HEAD request), card image, show.
